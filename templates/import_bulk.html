Bulk import

Two ways to fill your library quickly. Paste as many recipe links as you like, or paste one listing page and it'll import every recipe linked from it.

Listing pages worth trying

  • hellofresh.co.uk/recipes/most-popular-recipes
  • hellofresh.co.uk/recipes/quick-meals
  • hellofresh.co.uk/recipes/search?q=chicken

It waits a second between recipes, so a large import takes a while — roughly a second each. Re-importing a recipe you already have refreshes it rather than duplicating it.
